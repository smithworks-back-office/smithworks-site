What happens every pay period

With SmithWorks Back Office™, your records are kept and filings are done. No guessing, no hassle.

  1. Your crew clocks in on their phones from the job site, tagged to the job. You approve the hours.
  2. I calculate wages and overtime.
  3. You approve.
  4. Employees get paid by direct deposit.
  5. Your federal and state payroll taxes are paid.

New hire? I help with new hire paperwork and W-9 collection so you’re set for 1099s and W-2s at year’s end, well before the January deadline.

What I don’t do: tax returns. Your CPA keeps your taxes, and I make sure payroll never gives them (or you) a surprise.

The SmithWorks Back Office difference

What I offer that other payroll specialists don’t:

  • Hands-off payroll for owners. I don’t sell you software to run. Your crew taps in and out on their phones, you tap “Approve,” and I do the rest.
  • Cleaner wage reporting. Your bookkeeper and CPA get clean numbers, and you are ready when the workers’ comp auditor calls.
  • Labor cost by job, not by guess. Hours come in tagged to the job the crew was on. Your one-page monthly report shows what each job really cost you in wages, so you bid the next one with real numbers.
  • Threshold monitoring. I watch headcounts that trigger new rules and subs who start to look like employees. You hear from me before you hear from the state.
